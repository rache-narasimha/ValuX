ValuX privacy policy

SLN Consultancy & Engineering Services operates ValuX as a private workspace for property reports and work assignments. This policy explains how SLN handles information in ValuX, including its WhatsApp integration.

Information we use

We use account details such as your name, user ID, role, verified email, and optional profile photo to run the workspace. We use authentication records, session cookies, and security logs to sign users in, recover accounts, and protect access. Your browser also stores settings such as appearance and local practice drafts.

Workspace records can include applicant and property details, institution and branch information, site photos, reports, work history, and payment records. We use this information to prepare and deliver reports, manage assignments and payments, and maintain a record of the work.

WhatsApp verification and alerts

When WhatsApp is enabled and you register a number, we store it in encrypted form and send it to Meta’s WhatsApp Cloud API to deliver your verification code. Your verified number can identify your account at sign-in. We record your verification and alert preferences, process delivery status updates, and handle opt-out replies.

Assignment alerts are optional. If you opt in, an alert may include the report type, institution and branch, assignment and due dates, a masked applicant name, and a link that requires you to sign in. Full applicant names, property details, financial information, and report files remain in the workspace.

Use My Account in ValuX to replace your number or turn off assignment alerts. You can also reply STOP to the ValuX WhatsApp sender to turn off alerts. WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy policy.

Access, service providers and retention

Workspace access is limited by account permissions. Authorized staff may share reports with the institutions for which the work was prepared. Service providers used for hosting, storage, email and WhatsApp process the information needed to provide those services.

We keep information as needed to operate ValuX, maintain work and security records, and meet applicable obligations. Opting out changes your alert preference. Requests about the underlying account or work records are handled separately.

Privacy requests and contact

To request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to ask a privacy question, email support.valux@gmail.com. We may verify your identity before acting and may need to retain information for work records or legal obligations.
